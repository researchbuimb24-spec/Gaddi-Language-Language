# Sources and Bibliographical Assessment for Gaddi

This document provides a comprehensive survey and quality assessment of credible sources for the Gaddi language (*Bharmauri / Gaddiyali*), with a rigorous distinction between documentation of **Jammu & Kashmir (J&K)** varieties and **Himachal Pradesh (HP)** varieties.

---

## 1. Geographical Variety Classification

To avoid the linguistic error of merging distinct regional varieties without empirical evidence, all sources and lexical data in this library are categorized into distinct geographical tiers:

| Variety Identifier | Region | Primary Districts / Tehsils | Linguistic Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `jk_kathua_bani` | Jammu & Kashmir | Kathua (Bani, Duggan, Dhaggar, Billawar, Basohli) | High-altitude border with Chamba; transhumance corridor; intense contact with Pahari Dogri. |
| `jk_udhampur_ramnagar` | Jammu & Kashmir | Udhampur (Ramnagar, Dudu-Basantgarh, Chenani, Pachound) | High hill tracts; transhumance routes; contact with Dogri and Gojri. |
| `jk_doda_bhadarwah` | Jammu & Kashmir | Doda / Kishtwar (Bhadarwah, Assar, Saraj/Saraz, Marmat) | Chenab valley; contact with Bhaderwahi, Siraji, Kashmiri. |
| `jk_general` | Jammu & Kashmir | Undifferentiated J&K Gaddi | J&K-wide demographic or state-level documentation. |
| `hp_chamba_bharmaur` | Himachal Pradesh | Chamba (Bharmaur / Gaderan, Budhil valley) | The historical heartland of Bharmauri Gaddi. |
| `hp_kangra_palampur` | Himachal Pradesh | Kangra (Palampur foothills: Saperu, Banuri, Kandbari) | Kangra dialect of Gaddi documented by Kumari et al. (2026). |
| `hp_mandi_kullu` | Himachal Pradesh | Mandi, Kullu peripheral pockets | Peripheral varieties requiring fresh description. |
| `comparative_historical` | Pan-regional | Historical British surveys (LSI, Bailey) | Classical Western Pahari records; baseline comparative data. |

## 2. Bibliographical Survey and Reliability Tiers

### Tier 1: Peer-Reviewed Descriptive Linguistics and Technical Grammars

#### 1. Kumari, P., Mehta, S., Nair, A., Nayak, A., Roy, Y., & Sharma, V. (2026)
- **Title:** *A Grammar of Gaddi*
- **Publisher:** UCL Press, London (*Grammars of World and Minority Languages*, Vol. 5).
- **Identifier:** DOI: `10.14324/111.9781800085886` / OAPEN ID: `10220782`.
- **License:** Open Access under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**.
- **Geographic Variety Documented:** **`hp_kangra_palampur`** (Specifically the villages of Saperu, Banuri, and Kandbari near Palampur, Kangra district, HP).
- **Reliability:** **Very High (Gold Standard for Kangra Gaddi)**. Meticulous phonetics, phonology, morphological paradigms, syntactic descriptions, and glossed texts using the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA).
- **Critical Caveat for J&K Project:** The authors explicitly state in their introduction that while their grammar describes the variety spoken around Palampur, the varieties spoken in **Chamba, Mandi, Kullu, Punjab, and Jammu & Kashmir require fresh, dedicated descriptions**. Consequently, this source serves as a **comparative reference only** and must never be treated as direct evidence for J&K Gaddi lexical or grammatical forms without independent J&K attestation.

#### 2. Mehta, Shreya (2016, 2021)
- **Titles:**
  - *Verbal Inflection in Gaddi: A Paradigm-Based Approach* (M.Phil. Dissertation, Centre for Linguistics, Jawaharlal Nehru University, New Delhi, 2016).
  - *Tense, Aspect, and Mood in Gaddi and Neighboring Himalayan Languages* (Ph.D. Dissertation, Centre for Linguistics, JNU, 2021).
- **Geographic Variety Documented:** `hp_kangra_palampur`.
- **Reliability:** **Very High**. Deep theoretical and empirical analysis of verbal inflection, tense-aspect-mood (TAM) paradigms, and agreement in Gaddi.

### Tier 2: Historical and Macro-Linguistic Surveys

#### 3. Grierson, Sir George Abraham (1916)
- **Title:** *Linguistic Survey of India (LSI)*, Vol. IX: *Indo-Aryan Family, Central Group*, Part IV: *Specimens of the Pahari Languages and Gujuri*.
- **Publisher:** Office of the Superintendent of Government Printing, Calcutta.
- **License:** **Public Domain**.
- **Geographic Variety Documented:** **`hp_chamba_bharmaur`** (Bharmauri / Gadi) and comparative notes on neighboring Western Pahari varieties (Chambeali, Churahi, Pangwali, Bhaderwahi).
- **Reliability:** **High for historical comparative linguistics**. Provides standard word lists, grammatical sketches, and translated specimens (Parable of the Prodigal Son) in both Takri and Devanagari transliteration.
- **Caveat:** Data collected over a century ago via colonial intermediaries. Phonetic transcription lacks modern IPA precision; vocabulary reflects archaic or translation-influenced registers.

#### 4. Bailey, Rev. Thomas Grahame (1908)
- **Title:** *The Languages of the Northern Himalayas: Being Studies in the Grammar of Twenty-Six Himalayan Dialects*.
- **Publisher:** Royal Asiatic Society, London (Prize Publication Fund, Vol. XII).
- **Additional Reference:** Bailey's linguistic appendix in the *Kangra District Gazetteer* (1904).
- **License:** **Public Domain**.
- **Geographic Variety Documented:** Western Pahari languages of Chamba and Kangra.
- **Reliability:** **High historical value**. Early pioneer in recording tones and pitch phenomena in Western Pahari.

### Tier 3: Academic Studies, Fieldwork, and Government Resources in Jammu & Kashmir

#### 5. Census of India (2011, 2001)
- **Data Series:** *C-16: Population by Mother Tongue* & *Special Tables for Scheduled Tribes*.
- **Publisher:** Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner, India (Ministry of Home Affairs, New Delhi).
- **Key Findings for J&K:**
  - **2011 Census:** 24,161 mother-tongue speakers of Gaddi/Bharmauri recorded in Jammu & Kashmir. Total Gaddi Scheduled Tribe population in J&K: 46,489.
  - **2001 Census:** 13,349 mother-tongue speakers in J&K.
  - **Geographic Spread:** Concentrations in Kathua, Udhampur, Doda, Ramban, Kishtwar, and Reasi.
- **Reliability:** **High demographic authority**. Confirms substantial native speaker presence in J&K, while indicating language-shift pressures (ST population exceeds mother-tongue reporting).

#### 6. Kumar, Banti (2019)
- **Title:** "The Gaddi: A Jammu and Kashmir Himalayan Tribe"
- **Published in:** *Trends in Sociology, Psychology and Anthropology*, Vol. 1, AkiNik Publications, New Delhi.
- **Geographic Variety Documented:** **`jk_general`** / **`jk_kathua_bani`**.
- **Reliability:** **Moderate to High (Sociological/Anthropological)**. Documents J&K Gaddi social organization, transhumance routes, seasonal migratory settlements (*Hadwari*), and pastoral vocabulary.

#### 7. Chandar, Subhash (2024)
- **Title:** "Socio-cultural Analysis of Gaddi Tribe - A Sociological Study of Saraj Area in Jammu Region"
- **Published in:** *Antrocom Online Journal of Anthropology* / *IJFMR*.
- **Geographic Variety Documented:** **`jk_doda_bhadarwah`** (Saraj / Saraz area of Chenab Valley, Jammu).
- **Reliability:** **Moderate (Sociological)**. Provides localized ethnographic detail for the northernmost Gaddi settlements in J&K and contact with Kashmiri/Sarazi.

#### 8. Chakraborty, Arka (2024)
- **Title:** "Blessed by Lord Shiva: The Gaddi Tribe of Jammu and Kashmir"
- **Publisher:** Jammu & Kashmir Arts Foundation.
- **Geographic Variety Documented:** **`jk_general`**.
- **Reliability:** **Moderate**. Documents religious traditions, the Shaivite *Nawala* / *Shiv Nuala* ritual, pastoral economy, and educational challenges in J&K.

#### 9. Nirmohi, Prof. Shiv (Padma Shri Awardee)
- **Works:** *Duggar Ka Itihas*, *Saraz: Lok Aur Parampara*, *Duggar Ki Lok Gathayen*.
- **Publisher:** Various regional academic presses (Jammu).
- **Geographic Variety Documented:** **`jk_general`** / **`jk_kathua_bani`** / **`jk_udhampur_ramnagar`**.
- **Reliability:** **High Cultural & Folkloric Value**. Unmatched documentation of Jammu tribal folklore, local pastoral terminologies, and oral song genres.

#### 10. Jammu & Kashmir Academy of Art, Culture and Languages (JKAACL)
- **Resources:** Documentation of tribal festivals, folk ballad collections (*Ainchali* / *Anchli*), and folk theater.
- **Geographic Variety Documented:** `jk_general`.
- **Reliability:** **Moderate (Cultural Documentation)**. Rich source for authentic cultural and liturgical poetry, though linguistic glossing is rarely provided.

#### 11. Central Institute of Indian Languages (CIIL, Mysore) & LDC-IL
- **Resources:** *Bharmauri/Gaddi Parallel Text Corpus* and Western Pahari language surveys.
- **Publisher:** Central Institute of Indian Languages, Ministry of Education, Government of India.
- **Reliability:** **High**. Digital corpora and phonetic surveys for Indian minority languages.

### Tier 4: Community, Digital, and Crowdsourced Glossaries (Provisional Only)

#### 12. Digital Glossaries (Glosbe, Wiktionary, Independent Cultural Blogs)
- **Reliability:** **Low / Provisional**.
- **Project Rule:** Crowdsourced entries must NEVER be entered into the library as verified facts. They may only be logged in a quarantine review table to inform directed fieldwork or academic verification.
